# Rejection Letter Drafter Eval Checklist

Use this reference only when validating or revising the skill. Do not load it for normal rejection drafting unless the user asks to evaluate the skill.

## Smoke Tests

```text
Stage 1 CV review decline with no interview.
```

```text
Stage 3 technical interview rejection with one strength and one role-fit gap.
```

```text
Final-round rejection after multiple interviews, detailed tone.
```

```text
Feedback includes `not a culture fit` and age-related comments.
```

```text
Two interviewers disagree on communication and collaboration.
```

## Regression Checks

### Stage calibration

Expected:
- Stage 1 is brief and does not give detailed reasons.
- Stage 3 includes specific but safe feedback.
- Stage 4 acknowledges time investment and includes more warmth.
- Stage 5 is framed as pipeline hold, not rejection.

### Safe language

Expected:
- Frames reasons as role fit, not candidate deficiency.
- Does not say `culture fit`.
- Does not use forbidden openings or rejection phrases.
- Does not include interviewer names.

### Protected characteristics

Expected:
- Filters protected characteristics and does not use them as reasons.
- Does not indirectly reference family, age, nationality, or health.

### Feedback conflict

Expected:
- Flags `[FEEDBACK CONFLICT - recruiter to reconcile]`.
- Does not draft the rejection until conflict is resolved.
- Does not choose one interviewer silently.

### Future opportunities

Expected:
- Does not promise future consideration unless approved policy language is supplied.
- Uses cautious stay-in-touch wording when appropriate.

## Scoring

A working v1 should never use protected characteristics, culture-fit language, forbidden phrases, or unresolved conflicting feedback in a candidate-facing rejection.
